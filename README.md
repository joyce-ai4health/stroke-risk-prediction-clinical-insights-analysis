# Stroke Risk Prediction & Clinical Insights Analysis

## Project Overview

Healthcare data science project focused on identifying demographic, lifestyle, and clinical factors associated with stroke occurrence. Using patient health data, this project applies exploratory data analysis, predictive analytics, and interactive dashboards to support stroke risk assessment and early intervention strategies.

The project leverages Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI to uncover stroke risk patterns, develop predictive models, and generate actionable healthcare insights.

---

## Business Problem

Stroke is one of the leading causes of death and long-term disability worldwide. Early identification of individuals at high risk can improve preventive care and reduce adverse health outcomes.

Healthcare providers need data-driven tools to identify key risk factors, support clinical decision-making, and improve patient risk stratification.

This project aims to analyze patient demographic, lifestyle, and medical characteristics to better understand stroke occurrence and support preventive healthcare strategies.

## Dataset Information

### Dataset Name

Stroke Prediction Dataset

### Source

Kaggle

### Dataset Description

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), stroke is the second leading cause of death globally.

This dataset contains demographic, lifestyle, and clinical information used to predict whether a patient is likely to experience a stroke.

### Key Features

* Gender
* Age
* Hypertension status
* Heart disease status
* Marital status
* Work type
* Residence type
* Average glucose level
* Body Mass Index (BMI)
* Smoking status
* Stroke occurrence indicator

## Project Objectives

* Identify factors associated with stroke occurrence
* Analyze demographic and lifestyle risk patterns
* Evaluate the impact of hypertension and heart disease
* Explore relationships between BMI, glucose levels, and stroke
* Build predictive models for stroke risk assessment
* Develop interactive clinical insights dashboards

---

## Key Business Questions

1. Which factors are most strongly associated with stroke?
2. How does age affect stroke occurrence?
3. What is the relationship between hypertension and stroke risk?
4. How do glucose levels and BMI influence stroke outcomes?
5. Does smoking history significantly affect stroke occurrence?
6. Which demographic groups are at higher risk?
7. Can stroke occurrence be predicted using patient health indicators?
